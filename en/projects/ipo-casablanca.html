Performance of IPOs on the Casablanca Stock Exchange

Internship · Casablanca Stock Exchange, Market Development Department · July 2026

9 IPOs from 2020 to 2025: +31.5% six-month outperformance versus the MASI, linked to oversubscription (r = 0.77), but fragile without the largest deal.

Simulator (French interface) Code (GitHub) Report (PDF, French)

Question

Did recent Moroccan IPOs outperform the market, and which factors explain their performance? A question that matters to the Market Development Department when supporting future issuers.

Method

  1. Collection and cleaning of prices and offering data for the 9 IPOs.
  2. Buy-and-hold abnormal returns (BHAR) versus the MASI at day 1, 1, 3, 6 and 12 months.
  3. Wilcoxon test on the median, Pearson and Spearman correlations with oversubscription and amount raised.
  4. Backtest of a systematic participation strategy, 7-sheet Excel model.
  5. Live follow-up of the T2S Group Holding IPO, from subscription to the first trading sessions.

The six-month outperformance is marginally significant (p = 0.074) and loses significance without SGTM. Oversubscription appears to be the best indicator: r = 0.77 (p = 0.015) across 9 IPOs, and 0.93 without SGTM.

Limitations and next steps

  • Only nine IPOs: tests have low power and a single stock can change the conclusion.
  • The MASI benchmark controls neither for size nor for sector; a matched-portfolio benchmark would be more robust.
